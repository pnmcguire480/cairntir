# 1.14.1: the first audience capture works on an existing store

Status: **PREPARED, NOT PUBLISHED**. Prepared 2026-11-13. The maintainer has
approved publication; the tag is created only after this preparation merges.

## Decision

The independent evaluation of published 1.14.0 found one Tier 2 defect on
13 November 2026. The fix merged the same day in #176. The maintainer then said:
"Publish 1.14.1."

The number is 1.14.1 because the [release policy](../release-cadence.md) says a
patch is "fixes without a new capability". This release contains one fix and no
new capability. Work that adds capability (the R12, Q04 and C50 packets in #175)
is not in it and waits for a later minor release.

2.0.0 stays reserved for the finished roadmap.

## Contents

One change to the installed package since the `v1.14.0` tag. The
[changelog](https://github.com/pnmcguire480/cairntir/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
has the full wording.

Fixed:

- `cairntir audience capture` saves the first audience on a store that already
  holds memories (#176). In 1.14.0 it stopped with
  `cannot read audience revision: no such table: cairntir_audience_revisions`
  on any existing store without audience history, including every store created
  by 1.13.0. The failed capture saved nothing, so no data was lost.

Also on `main` since the tag, with no change to the installed package: the
1.14.0 publication record (#174).

Roadmap: unchanged by this release, 26 of 35 component criteria complete and 9
open (Q04, R12, R13, R14, I02, I03, I05, C50, R16).

## What an existing user must do

Upgrade. There is no database schema change and no change to the embedding
identity since 1.13.0, so no reindex is needed. Anyone whose first
`cairntir audience capture` failed on 1.14.0 can run the same command again
after upgrading; nothing from the failed attempt needs cleaning up.

`cairntir audience status` on a store that has never held an audience still
reports the typed "no such table" error until the first capture. That behavior
is pinned by a frozen 1.13.0 acceptance test and was left as it is.

## Independent evaluation of 1.14.0

An independent evaluator on a second computer tested published 1.14.0 on
13 November 2026, in four stages, using tests the builder has never seen. The
builder read the stage 3 and stage 4 reports directly. The stage 1 verdict was
relayed by the maintainer and the stage 2 result is taken from its restatement
in the stage 3 report and from #176; those two report files had not reached the
builder when this record was written.

- **Stage 1, the frozen 1.13.0 tests rerun on 1.14.0:** PASS. The replay defect
  found in 1.13.0 is fixed, with no Tier 1 or Tier 2 regression.
- **Stage 2, what is new in 1.14.0:** FAIL on one Tier 2 finding, the audience
  capture defect above, with no Tier 1 finding and no data loss.
- **Stage 3, the 29 promises not tested on 1.13.0:** 22 PASS, 1 FAIL (the same
  audience finding, carried from stage 2) and 6 NOT TESTED. No new Tier 1 or
  Tier 2 finding. The stage verdict is FAIL because of the carried finding.
- **Stage 4, deliberately broken copies of the package:** this stage rates the
  evaluator's tests, not the product. Of 43 areas, 36 breaks were caught, 6 were
  missed and 1 was not tested. No product defect follows from a miss, but the
  earlier PASS in each missed area is weaker evidence: governance attribution,
  attachment corruption that keeps the length, the pre-reindex backup, portable
  correction links, one managed-action check and one terminal-task check.

The evaluator has not rerun against this fix.

## Known limits

- The evaluator's six untested promises: native Codex hosting of managed
  sessions, calendar generation, resource verification, the native Obsidian
  plugin, the native Blender add-on, and the error path under a very long
  working directory.
- Three Tier 3 findings from stage 3 are open and not fixed in this release:
  the study export's "current" heading does not follow derivation status; the
  calendar guide gives no complete input schema; and the resource verification
  guide points to `plans/resources-q05-r1.md`, which does not exist at the tag.
- The evaluation is not third-party custody. One person uses both computers, the
  builder supplied the public documents copy and one interface contract, and the
  evaluator reported small procedural deviations of its own.
- Everything listed under known limits in the [1.14.0 record](v1.14.0.md) still
  applies: Dream's timing sensitivity, coverage of 87 to 88% against the 90%
  still owed, `cairntir study-deliver` proven against a simulated notebook only,
  and nine open roadmap items.
