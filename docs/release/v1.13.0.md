# 1.13.0: the work toward 2.0, published under a 1.x number

Status: **PUBLISHED** to PyPI and GitHub on 2026-11-12. Prepared 2026-11-09.

## Decision

On 9 November 2026 the maintainer decided to publish the work toward 2.0 now,
under a 1.x number, so that people can use and test it while the remaining
roadmap is finished. In their words: "it can be a v1.x.x for now before 2 is
finished, i want the new features being tested by users early." 2.0.0 stays
reserved for the finished roadmap.

The same day the maintainer set the coverage floor for this release at 87%: "87%
is all we need, thats my compromise, ill go back at another date and 90%
everything after." The alpha previews used an 85% floor set on 4 November. Runs
measured against the earlier 90% floor keep their original failing results in the
[CI record](v2-alpha-ci.md) and the [finish-line checklist](v2-finish-line.md).

## Contents

Everything in the GitHub-only previews 2.0.0a1 to 2.0.0a4, plus the wing
recognition fixes listed under 1.13.0 in the changelog. [ALPHA.md](https://github.com/pnmcguire480/cairntir/blob/main/ALPHA.md)
lists the features, which of them are experimental, and the open roadmap items.

## What an existing user must do

Upgrading a store from 1.12 or earlier needs one manual step. Semantic search and
saving new memories are refused until the index is rebuilt with
`cairntir reindex --yes --backup /absolute/path/to/new-backup.db`, run with every
client stopped. Reading by id and `cairntir status` keep working before that. The
[upgrade guide](../embedding-artifacts.md) has the full procedure.

## Known limits at preparation time

- The independent evaluator failed 2.0.0a2 on a retry defect. That defect was
  fixed in 2.0.0a4, and the evaluator has not rerun its tests since.
- Coverage is 87-88%, below the 90% target that is still owed.
- The upgrade, restore and rollback have been exercised on two Windows machines.
  Other platforms have automated coverage only.
- GitHub's CodeQL results check reported 5 error-level alerts in acceptance test
  files. Each was reviewed and is not a defect; they were dismissed with written
  reasons rather than by editing the registered test sources.
- Eleven dated roadmap follow-ups and the remaining release gates are open.
